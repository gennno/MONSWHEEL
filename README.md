# 🚛 Monswheel

**Real-Time A2B Service Monitoring System for Mining Operations**

Monswheel is a web-based operational monitoring system designed to streamline and monitor A2B service activities in mining operations. The system provides real-time visibility into unit service progress, automatically calculates planned and actual downtime, and displays live service information on operational dashboards and videotrons to support maintenance teams and operational decision-making.

# 📖 About the Project

Monswheel is a digital monitoring platform developed to support **A2B (Arrival to Ready-for-Use) service operations** in the mining industry. Instead of manually tracking maintenance activities, operators can monitor every service unit in real time through a centralized dashboard.

The system records every operational timestamp throughout the service workflow and automatically performs downtime calculations, allowing maintenance teams to monitor service performance accurately and efficiently.

In addition to the web dashboard, Monswheel also provides a **videotron display** that presents live service information, including unit status, entry time, elapsed service duration, and readiness, enabling supervisors and operational teams to monitor ongoing activities at a glance.
